Local paper simulation. Stocks use USD and crypto uses a separate USDT balance; USDT is not converted to USD. Feed availability depends on the provider. No orders are sent to a broker or exchange. The default strategy uses research rules; an optional forex ML veto is unvalidated. No trading edge is verified. Polling stops cannot guarantee a loss limit. Rollover/swap costs and exchange liquidity are not modeled. Pause new entries with python -m brobs.fx_runner --pause; keep the runner active to evaluate stops.
